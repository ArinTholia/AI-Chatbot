# SRMIST B.Tech Departments and Specializations

## School of Computing
The School of Computing at SRMIST is one of the largest in India with over 8,000 students.

### Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- Core CSE covers data structures, algorithms, operating systems, database management, and software engineering.
- Faculty strength: 120+ professors including 35 PhDs.
- Labs: Advanced Computing Lab, Cloud Computing Lab, Cybersecurity Lab.
- Research areas: Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision.

### CSE - Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Specialization in deep learning, neural networks, reinforcement learning, and computer vision.
- Dedicated AI Lab with GPU workstations (NVIDIA A100).
- Industry partnerships with Google, Microsoft, and IBM for guest lectures and internships.
- Students work on real-world AI projects in final year capstone.

### CSE - Data Science
- Focus on big data analytics, statistical modeling, data visualization, and business intelligence.
- Curriculum includes Python, R, Hadoop, Spark, Tableau, and Power BI.

### CSE - Cyber Security
- Covers ethical hacking, cryptography, network security, and digital forensics.
- Students participate in national-level CTF (Capture The Flag) competitions.

### Information Technology (IT)
- Covers web technologies, mobile app development, cloud computing, and DevOps.
- Active open-source contribution community.

## School of Electronics and Communication
### Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
- Focus on VLSI design, embedded systems, signal processing, and IoT.
- Labs: VLSI Design Lab, Microprocessor Lab, Communication Systems Lab, IoT Innovation Lab.
- Faculty strength: 80+ professors.
- Industry collaborations with Texas Instruments, Qualcomm, and Intel.

### ECE - Internet of Things (IoT)
- Specialization in smart systems, sensor networks, edge computing, and industrial IoT.

## School of Mechanical Engineering
### Mechanical Engineering
- Covers thermodynamics, manufacturing processes, robotics, and automobile engineering.
- Labs: CAD/CAM Lab, Robotics Lab, 3D Printing Lab, Wind Tunnel Lab.
- Active SAE (Society of Automotive Engineers) student chapter.
- Students build Formula Student race cars and participate in national competitions.

### Mechatronics
- Interdisciplinary program combining mechanical, electronics, and computer science.
- Focus on automation, control systems, and smart manufacturing.

## School of Bioengineering
### Biotechnology
- Covers genetic engineering, bioinformatics, pharmaceutical biotechnology, and environmental biotech.
- BSL-2 certified laboratory for advanced microbiology research.
- Research collaborations with CSIR, ICMR, and international universities.

## School of Civil Engineering
### Civil Engineering
- Covers structural engineering, environmental engineering, transportation, and geotechnical engineering.
- Advanced Concrete Technology Lab, Environmental Engineering Lab, Survey Lab.
- Students participate in building sustainable housing competitions.
