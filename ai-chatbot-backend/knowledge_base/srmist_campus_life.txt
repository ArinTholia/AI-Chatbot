# SRMIST Campus Life, Clubs, and Cultural Activities

## Cultural Festivals
### Aaruush - National Level Techno-Management Fest
- One of the largest technical festivals in South India, held annually in September/October.
- Events include hackathons, robotics competitions, coding contests, paper presentations, and workshops.
- Past sponsors include Google, Microsoft, Amazon, TCS, and Red Bull.
- Attracts over 15,000 participants from 200+ colleges across India.

### Milan - Cultural Fest
- Annual cultural extravaganza featuring music, dance, drama, fashion shows, and literary events.
- Celebrity performances from Bollywood and regional artists.
- Inter-college competitions with cash prizes.

## Student Clubs and Chapters

### Technical Clubs
- **Coding Club (CodeSRM)**: Weekly coding contests, DSA workshops, competitive programming training. Prepares students for ICPC and Google Code Jam.
- **AI/ML Club**: Hands-on workshops on TensorFlow, PyTorch. Kaggle competition participation.
- **Robotics Club (Robocon SRM)**: Builds robots for ABU Robocon Asia-Pacific competition. Has won multiple national awards.
- **Cybersecurity Club**: Organizes CTF events, ethical hacking workshops, and bug bounty programs.
- **Open Source Club**: Contributes to open-source projects. Mentors students for Google Summer of Code (GSoC).
- **Blockchain Club**: Workshops on Ethereum, Solidity, and decentralized applications.
- **IoT Lab Club**: Builds smart campus solutions using Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and ESP32.

### Cultural and Social Clubs
- **Music Club (Raga SRM)**: Western and Indian classical music performances and training.
- **Dance Club (Nritya SRM)**: Classical, contemporary, hip-hop, and folk dance teams.
- **Drama Club (Enact SRM)**: Theatre performances, street plays, and inter-college drama competitions.
- **Photography Club**: Campus photography walks, photo exhibitions, and workshops.
- **NSS (National Service Scheme)**: Community service, blood donation drives, rural development programs.
- **Rotaract Club**: Social service projects, leadership development, and community outreach.

### Sports Clubs
- **Cricket Club**: Participates in inter-university tournaments. Has produced state-level players.
- **Football Club**: Regular league matches and inter-college tournaments.
- **Basketball Club**: Competes in South Zone university championships.
- **Athletics Club**: Track and field training with professional coaches.
- **E-Sports Club**: Competitive gaming in Valorant, CS2, and League of Legends. Organizes gaming tournaments.

## Sports Facilities
- Olympic-sized swimming pool.
- Indoor badminton, table tennis, and squash courts.
- Football ground, cricket ground, and athletics track (400m synthetic).
- Fully equipped gymnasium with modern equipment.
- Basketball and volleyball courts.
- Yoga and meditation center.

## Library - Central Library
- One of the largest university libraries in India.
- Over 1,50,000 books and 5,000+ journals (both print and digital).
- Digital access to IEEE, Springer, Elsevier, and ACM research databases.
- 24/7 reading rooms during exam season.
- Separate discussion rooms for group study.
- Computer terminals with internet access.
- E-book and audiobook collection.
