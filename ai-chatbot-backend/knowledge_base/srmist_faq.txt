# SRMIST Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Admission Related

### Can I get admission without SRMJEEE?
Yes, a limited number of seats are available under Management Quota. Admission under management quota is based on 12th marks and a personal interview. However, the fees are higher than for SRMJEEE-admitted students.

### Is there lateral entry for diploma holders?
Yes, SRMIST offers lateral entry admission directly into the 2nd year of B.Tech programs for diploma holders and B.Sc graduates. Admission is based on marks obtained in the qualifying examination. Approximately 10% of total seats are reserved for lateral entry.

### What is the age limit for admission?
There is no specific age limit for B.Tech admission at SRMIST. However, candidates must have completed 12th standard in the year of admission or the preceding year.

### Can I change my branch after admission?
Branch change is possible after the first year, subject to availability of seats and academic performance. Students must have a minimum CGPA of 8.0 (out of 10) in the first two semesters. Branch change is granted on merit basis and is limited to 5% of seats in each branch.

## Academic Related

### What is the attendance requirement?
A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory in each subject. Students with less than 75% attendance will not be allowed to sit for end-semester examinations. Medical leave with valid certificates is considered for attendance shortage.

### How is the grading system?
SRMIST follows a 10-point CGPA grading system:
- O (Outstanding): 10 points (91-100 marks)
- A+ (Excellent): 9 points (81-90 marks)
- A (Very Good): 8 points (71-80 marks)
- B+ (Good): 7 points (61-70 marks)
- B (Average): 6 points (56-60 marks)
- C (Satisfactory): 5 points (50-55 marks)
- F (Fail): 0 points (below 50 marks)

### Are internships mandatory?
Yes, internships are mandatory in the 6th and 8th semesters. The Training and Placement department helps students secure internships at top companies. Many internships are paid (stipend ranges from Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 50,000 per month).

### Is there a semester exchange program?
Yes, SRMIST has semester exchange agreements with universities in USA, UK, Australia, Germany, Singapore, and South Korea. Students can spend one semester abroad while earning credits that transfer back to SRMIST.

## Hostel Related

### Is hostel accommodation guaranteed?
Hostel accommodation is available for all first-year students. From second year onwards, it is subject to availability. Preference is given to students from outside Tamil Nadu.

### What are the hostel timings and rules?
- Hostel gate closes at 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:00 PM on weekends.
- Visitors are allowed only in the common area during visiting hours (4 PM - 7 PM on weekends).
- Alcohol, smoking, and ragging are strictly prohibited and are grounds for immediate expulsion.
- Students can go home on weekends with prior permission from the warden.

### Can I get a single room?
Yes, single AC rooms with attached bathrooms are available at Rs. 2,25,000 per year. These rooms are limited and are allotted on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Placement Related

### When do placements start?
Campus placements typically start in August of the final year (7th semester) and continue until March. Pre-placement offers (PPOs) from internships are announced from June onwards.

### What is the minimum CGPA required for placements?
Most companies require a minimum CGPA of 6.0. Top companies like Google, Amazon, and Microsoft typically require 7.5+. There is no active backlog allowed at the time of placement.

### Do all students get placed?
SRMIST achieves a placement rate of approximately 95% for registered students. Students who do not get campus placements are supported with additional training and off-campus placement drives.

### What are the top packages offered?
- Highest domestic package (2025): Rs. 52 LPA (offered by a top tech company)
- Average package: Rs. 9.5 LPA
- Median package: Rs. 6.5 LPA
- Lowest package: Rs. 3.5 LPA

## Financial Related

### Are education loans available?
Yes, SRMIST has tie-ups with SBI, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, and other banks for education loans. The university provides all necessary documents for loan processing. Loans cover tuition fees, hostel fees, and other expenses.

### Can I pay fees in installments?
Yes, fees can be paid in two installments per year (semester-wise). Some students also arrange quarterly payment plans through the accounts department.

### Is there any fee refund policy?
If a student withdraws before the start of classes, the full fee (minus registration/processing charges) is refunded. After classes begin, refunds follow UGC guidelines (decreasing refund percentage based on time elapsed).
