# SRMIST Admission Process 2026

## SRMJEEE (SRM Joint Engineering Entrance Examination)

### About the Exam
SRMJEEE is the entrance examination conducted by SRM Institute of Science and Technology for admission to B.Tech programs. It is held in multiple phases (typically 3 phases) throughout April and June each year.

### Exam Pattern
- Mode: Computer-Based Test (Online)
- Duration: 2 hours 30 minutes
- Total Questions: 125 (MCQ format)
- Subjects and distribution:
  - Physics: 35 questions
  - Chemistry: 35 questions
  - Mathematics/Biology: 40 questions
  - English: 5 questions
  - Aptitude: 10 questions
- Marking: +3 for correct answer, No negative marking.
- Language: English only.

### Important Dates (2026 Approximate)
- Registration Opens: October 2025
- Registration Deadline: March 2026
- Phase 1 Exam: April 2026 (third week)
- Phase 2 Exam: June 2026 (first week)
- Results: Within 7-10 days after each phase
- Counselling: July 2026

### Registration Process
1. Visit the official SRMIST admission portal (www.srmist.edu.in).
2. Click on "Apply Now" and fill the online application form.
3. Upload passport size photograph and signature.
4. Pay the application fee of Rs. 1,200 (online payment via credit/debit card or net banking).
5. Download the admit card from the portal 7 days before the exam.
6. Carry the admit card and a valid government photo ID to the exam center.

### Exam Centers
SRMJEEE is conducted in 130+ cities across India, including all state capitals and major cities. International centers are available in Dubai and Muscat.

## Counselling Process
1. After results are declared, qualified candidates are called for counselling based on their rank.
2. Counselling is conducted online through the SRMIST portal.
3. Students choose their preferred branch/specialization based on available seats and their rank.
4. A counselling fee of Rs. 50,000 is payable at the time of seat confirmation (adjusted against first semester fees).
5. Document verification is done at the campus before the academic session begins.

### Documents Required for Counselling
- SRMJEEE Admit Card and Rank Card
- 10th and 12th mark sheets (originals and photocopies)
- Transfer Certificate from previous institution
- Migration Certificate (for students from other state boards)
- Community/Caste Certificate (if applicable)
- Passport-size photographs (6 copies)
- Aadhaar Card
- Medical fitness certificate

## Direct Admission (Management Quota)
A limited number of seats (approximately 30%) are available under management quota. Admission is based on 12th marks and a personal interview. Management quota fees are higher than regular admission fees.

## NRI/International Admissions
Separate seats are reserved for NRI and international students. Admission is based on SAT/equivalent scores or SRMJEEE performance. Fee structure for NRI students is in USD (approximately $6,000 per year for B.Tech programs).
