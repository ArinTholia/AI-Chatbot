# SRMIST Campus Facilities

## Transportation
### Bus Service
- SRMIST operates a fleet of 100+ buses covering all major areas of Chennai and suburbs.
- Bus routes cover areas including T. Nagar, Anna Nagar, Tambaram, Velachery, OMR, Porur, Guindy, and Chromepet.
- Bus fee: Approximately Rs. 30,000 to Rs. 45,000 per year depending on distance.
- Air-conditioned and non-AC buses available.
- GPS tracking available on the SRM Transport app for real-time bus location.

### Metro Connectivity
- The campus is well connected by Chennai Metro Rail. The nearest metro station is "SRM University" on the Blue Line.
- Free shuttle service from metro station to campus (every 10 minutes).

## Medical and Health Facilities
### SRM Medical College and Hospital
- A 1,200-bed multi-specialty hospital located on campus.
- 24/7 emergency services available for students and staff.
- Free basic medical consultation for all enrolled students.
- Ambulance service available on campus.
- Health insurance provided for all hostellers.

### Campus Health Center
- Primary health center inside the campus for first-aid and minor treatments.
- Full-time doctor and nurse on duty during college hours.
- Free medicines for common illnesses.

## Food and Dining
### Food Courts
- 4 food courts across the campus serving North Indian, South Indian, Chinese, and Continental cuisine.
- Prices range from Rs. 30 to Rs. 150 per meal.
- Vegetarian and non-vegetarian options available.
- Separate Jain food counter available.

### Cafeterias and Restaurants
- Subway, Dominos, CCD (Cafe Coffee Day), and local food chains on campus.
- 24/7 cafeteria in the hostel zone.
- Juice bars and snack shops in every academic block.

## Banking and ATMs
- State Bank of India (SBI) branch inside the campus.
- ATMs from SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank located at multiple points on campus.
- UPI and digital payment accepted at all campus outlets.

## Technology Infrastructure
### Wi-Fi
- Campus-wide high-speed Wi-Fi (1 Gbps backbone) available in all academic blocks, hostels, and common areas.
- Each student gets an individual login for internet access.
- Separate high-speed connection for research labs.

### Computer Labs
- Over 5,000 computers across all departments.
- Licensed software: MATLAB, AutoCAD, SolidWorks, ANSYS, Adobe Creative Suite.
- 24/7 access to computer labs for project work.

## Other Facilities
### Auditoriums
- T.P. Ganesan Auditorium: 3,000-seat capacity, used for major events and convocations.
- Mini auditoriums in each academic block (200-300 capacity).
- Open-air theatre for cultural performances.

### Gymnasium and Recreation
- State-of-the-art gymnasium with cardio and weight training equipment.
- Separate gym facilities for boys and girls hostels.
- Indoor games room with table tennis, carrom, and chess.

### Laundry
- Automated laundry service available in all hostels.
- Charges: Approximately Rs. 200-300 per month.
- Same-day delivery available.

### Shopping
- Stationery store, bookshop, and general store on campus.
- Photocopy and printing services in every academic block.
