# SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) - Admission Prospectus 2026

## About SRMIST
SRM Institute of Science and Technology (formerly known as SRM University) is one of the top ranking universities in India with over 52,000 full time students and more than 3200 faculty across all the campuses - Kattankulathur, Ramapuram, Vadapalani Campus – all in and around Chennai.

## B.Tech Programs and Fees (Annual)
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE): ₹ 4,50,000 per year
- CSE with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning: ₹ 4,50,000 per year
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE): ₹ 2,50,000 per year
- Mechanical Engineering: ₹ 2,50,000 per year
- Civil Engineering: ₹ 2,50,000 per year
- Biotechnology: ₹ 2,50,000 per year

## Eligibility Criteria
For B.Tech programs, candidates must have passed the 10+2 examination with a minimum of 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM). Admission is strictly based on the performance in the SRMJEEE (SRM Joint Engineering Entrance Examination) conducted by the institute.

## Hostel Facilities and Fees
SRMIST offers excellent hostel facilities for both boys and girls.
- Non-AC Room (3 Sharing): ₹ 1,15,000 per year
- AC Room (2 Sharing): ₹ 1,60,000 per year
- AC Room with attached bath (Single): ₹ 2,25,000 per year
Mess fees (including breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner) are approximately ₹ 65,000 per year and are mandatory for hostellers. The hostels provide 24/7 Wi-Fi, laundry services, and gym access.

## Placements
SRMIST has an outstanding placement record. The Career Centre facilitates campus interviews.
- Highest Package Offered (2025 batch): ₹ 52 Lakhs Per Annum (LPA)
- Average Package: ₹ 9.5 Lakhs Per Annum (LPA)
- Top Recruiters: Amazon, Microsoft, TCS, Infosys, Cognizant, Wipro, Google, IBM, Deloitte.
Over 800+ companies visit the campus every year, achieving a 95% placement rate for registered students.

## Scholarships
SRMIST offers several scholarships:
1. Founder's Scholarship: 100% tuition and hostel fee waiver for SRMJEEE top 100 rankers.
2. Merit Scholarship: 25% to 75% tuition fee waiver based on SRMJEEE rank (Ranks 101-3000).
3. Sports Scholarship: For students excelling in state/national level sports.
